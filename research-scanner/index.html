Structurally qualified — this scan

This is a continuously growing research database with an automated paper-trading simulation, built as two fully independent lifecycles. The research lifecycle answers "would today's scanner still discover this contract?" (ACTIVE → LEFT FILTER → RETURNED → EXPIRED) and is metadata only. The paper position answers "what would have happened after purchase?" — it enters at the first market-hours scan (entry = midpoint) and is tracked until one of its OWN rules fires (−30% initial, +25% trailing activation / 20% distance, 7-day time stop, DTE<14, expiration). Leaving the research filter never closes a paper trade; the tracker keeps re-quoting it. The two are never merged. Everything here is a simulation — not a real account, not a recommendation, no profit is claimed. Open Active Calls / Active Puts for live cards, Archive to search history, or Analytics for descriptive statistics.
